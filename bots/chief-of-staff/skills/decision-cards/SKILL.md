# Decision cards

## When to use it

Any time work waits on the captain: a product fork, a merge OK, a spend, a send, a "should we".

## Inputs and access

- The decision, the options you can see, and what each one costs.
- The queue of decisions already waiting (keep it in a file on your computer).

## Steps

1. Write the decision as one question the captain can answer without scrolling back.
2. Give two to four options. Put your recommendation first and mark it. Add one line per option on what it costs or risks.
3. If other decisions are waiting, number this one ("1 of 3") and hold the others.
4. Send it as a choice card when the app offers one. Otherwise send a numbered list and ask for one digit.
5. When the answer comes, record it with the date in your decisions file, act on it, then send the next card.

## Validate

One decision per message. The recommendation is first. Every option can be chosen as written, with no follow-up question needed.

## Return

```
1 of 3: <the question>
1. <recommended option> (recommended): <what it costs>
2. <option>: <what it costs>
3. Park it: <what waiting costs>
```

## Needs approval

The card is the approval. Never act on an option the captain did not pick.
