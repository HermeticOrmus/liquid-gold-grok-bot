# Guides

This page is the reading list beside the catalog: official guides and product docs, gotchas that change how a template or a bot behaves, posts and announcements, and community hubs. A row belongs here when it changes how someone installs, runs or judges an entry in this library. Each row records the date the source was read, one line in our own words on what it means for a Grok Bot user, and the [rubric](RUBRIC.md) stage or bot it bears on. A gotcha stays marked *inferred* until someone reproduces it on a live Grok Bot.
