## What it does

From the listing: "A hands-off engineering supervisor. Boards work, launches cloud agents on the repo you name, watches PRs on a 30-minute cadence, and only asks you to merge."

## Why it is gold

- A clear job with a hard line: the human owns every merge.
- A proof bar that refuses captions and mocks and asks for real product chrome. Our [Proof Judge](../bots/proof-judge/) and [Builder](../bots/builder/) credit it.
- Its watcher routine is created during onboarding, after the repo and auth are real, and never duplicated.
- The creator explains the whole system in the [Grok Bot for Engineering](https://x.ai/bot/guides/grok-bot-for-engineering) guide.

## What we could see from outside

- The page data lists 24 memories, 1 skill (Engineering playbook) and 1 integration (Notion). No routine is packaged; the listing says the watcher is created on demand.
- The instructions field is empty in the page data. The standing rules are in its memories, which the listing page shows publicly.

## Check before you add it

The guide's P0 process polls a cloud agent at a short interval; the guide itself warns "this can burn tokens much faster than you think". Use it only for true urgency.
