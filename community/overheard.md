## What it does

From the listing: "Watches Reddit, Hacker News, news sites, and X for third-party mentions of your name, brand, and URLs, then sends a short weekday digest when something clears the bar. Stays quiet on dead days and never posts on your behalf."

## Why it is gold

- Silent when nothing clears the bar, and it never posts: the two rules our [Night Watch](../bots/night-watch/) and [Pantry Researcher](../bots/pantry-researcher/) share.
- Its routine is created paused after the setup interview and runs only when the user says so.
