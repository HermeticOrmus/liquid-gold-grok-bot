## What it does

From the listing: "Inventories your SaaS spend from Ramp and bills, finds evidence-backed savings (unused seats, duplicates, cheaper alternatives), and drafts vendor counters for your review. Never spends, signs, or sends without you."

## Why it is gold

- Money work with the three hard refusals written into its one-line description: spend, sign, send.
- Its scoring skill says anything without dollars traced to live data "is a LEAD, not an opportunity": evidence before claims.
- Its vendor counter skill is "Draft only, never sends", and a separate skill re-reads every sheet write before it says done.
- Both routines ship disabled by default.
