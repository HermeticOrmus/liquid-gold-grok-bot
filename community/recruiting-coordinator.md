## What it does

From the listing: "Schedules interview loops, preps your interviewers, and chases what's stalled. Works from your calendar or a pasted list, and never emails a candidate without you."

## Why it is gold

- A clear job with its anti-jobs: "I don't source candidates, decide who to hire, or coach candidates."
- Its refusals name the consequential acts: "I never invent a candidate, an interviewer, a time, or feedback, and I never send, book, post, or reject without your yes."
- Data it refuses to keep: "Never store or infer age, a graduation year used as an age proxy, gender, race, nationality, religion, disability, health, pregnancy, marital or family status, or sexual orientation, and never let any of it into a packet, a note, a draft, or a debrief." Our [rubric](../RUBRIC.md) keeps private data out of shared text; this writes the rule for a hiring record.
- Shared places get less: "Notes hold job-related evidence only, and candidate details never go into a group channel."
- No guessed contact data: "Never guess an email, a phone number, or an employer."
- Evidence on ratings: its coaching routine "flags ratings written without evidence."
- Drafts only: its reply routine "leaves an unsent draft reply for each new candidate email so the user only edits and sends." Its first memory says "My routines stay off until you turn them on and they run in your timezone."

## What we could see from outside

- 9 integrations: slack, notion-workspace, linear, figma, Gmail, Google Calendar, Granola, Google Sheets, Ashby.
- 7 routines: "Daily hiring brief", "Evening interview prep", "Afternoon loop check", "Urgent thread check", "Candidate reply drafts", "Interview coaching review", "Weekly pipeline review".
- The instructions field is empty in the page data. The standing rules are in its memories.

## Cracks seen from outside

- Two routines, "Urgent thread check" and "Candidate reply drafts", run "Through the workday" with no stated interval. That is a standing listener on candidate mail, and no line says what a run spends.
- Ashby's line is "Search candidates, prep interviews, and manage pipeline tasks." The refusal list covers reject, but the page does not say whether moving a candidate's stage in the ATS waits for a yes.
- The notion-workspace line keeps "the hiring tracker, prep packets, and debrief summaries where the hiring team already looks." The group channel rule covers chat; the page does not say who can read that workspace.
- No routine summary states a quiet rule for a day with nothing booked or stalled.
