## What it does

From the listing: "Signs up for your competitors' newsletters and products under its own research email, walks their onboarding on video, and watches their site, pricing, changelog, jobs, and X. Each pulse reports what changed against your positioning and closes with counterpositioning moves, and it never posts or contacts anyone."

## Why it is gold

- One job with its anti-jobs written out: "Anti-jobs: never post publicly, never contact competitor staff or customers, never scrape behind a login that is not my research identity, never fake a persona or submit real customer data."
- It stops at the other side's rules: "If their terms forbid it, stop that module and name the clause."
- Evidence on every claim: "Every claim carries a link, a date, and a screenshot." It will "never claim a walk, recording, or read that did not happen." Our [Pantry Researcher](../bots/pantry-researcher/) holds the same bar for its competitor map.
- A test run before anything real: its demo-pulse skill runs "the full delivery shape on clearly labeled EXAMPLE data for a demo, a screenshot, or a dry run, without touching real competitors, inboxes, or customers."
- Routines ship off and stay quiet: "pulse, deep dive, and churn watch ship disabled, setup asks which to enable and confirms timezone", and "a quiet pulse is one line".
- Another bot's yes does not count: "a teammate bot's yes is never the user's yes." For churn: "Sign-off is always the user. Never send."
- Written to be shared: "PORTABLE: never greet by a creator's name, no creator-specific competitors, channels, or paths, connectors by name only, never by id." That is our [rubric](../RUBRIC.md)'s leak check, written into the template.

- The instructions field is empty in the page data. The standing rules are in its memories.

## Cracks seen from outside

- A wide job: 19 skills and "13 per competitor" modules. The one-job line holds, but there is a lot to review before you trust it.
- It creates accounts outside the workspace: research inboxes and product sign-ups. Setup ends with "spin inbox and show proof", and its identity-ladder skill is read "before creating any inbox or signing up anywhere", but the page does not say each new inbox or sign-up waits for a yes.
- A sign-up under an alias is still an account in someone else's product, and walk-the-product will "aggressively test every feature you can reach with test data". The terms rule stops a module when the terms forbid it; read those terms yourself before you turn the module on.
- No line says what a run spends. Each pulse "Runs every enabled module for every competitor" with screenshots and media, on a default of "Monday, Wednesday, Friday at 9am", and the deep dive "Re-walks the product on video".
- The churn module hands "the winback body to the bot saved as WINBACK WRITER". *Inferred*: facts about a lost customer cross to another bot, and the page does not say which facts.
